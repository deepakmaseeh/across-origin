Brand applications

How the identity lives on stationery, apparel, signage, vehicle and digital surfaces — from Brand Guidelines V2.0.

Stationery

Business cards 85×55 mm, A4 letterhead, DL envelopes — horizontal lockup preferred.

Apparel & merch

Polo, tee, cap, mug, notebook, book cover — emblem or mono lockup.

Signage & vehicle

Office sign, van wrap, social banner — clear space always.

Construction, clear space & usage

Clear space X
Equal to the height of the sun / origin point on all sides.

Construction & size

Mark is 1:1 — sun ≈ 22% of height, A-frame ≈ 78%.

Print: 20 mm wide
Digital: 80 px wide
Favicon: symbol-only at 16 / 32 / 48 px

Backgrounds

Use on white, light neutral, deep indigo (reverse), gold (reverse), charcoal (reverse), or photography with a clear quiet area.

Incorrect usage

Do not stretch or distort, change colors outside the palette, add glow/shadow/effects, rearrange mark and type, or place on busy backgrounds.
