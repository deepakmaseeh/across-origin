How the visual identity extends across physical stationery, apparel, merchandise, vehicle livery, and environmental signage.

Signage & Fleet

Construction, clear space & usage

Clear Space ‘X’
Equal to the outer diameter of the golden sun origin point on all four sides. No typography, icons, or trims may enter this boundary.

Construction & sizing ratios

The emblem mark is strictly 1:1 aspect ratio. The golden sun origin represents 22% of total height; the A-frame structural pathways occupy 78%.

Print minimum: 20 mm wide
Digital screen minimum: 80 px wide
Favicon / App icon: symbol-only at 16 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 256 px

Approved backgrounds

White, Light Neutral (#F3F5FA), Deep Indigo (reversed to pure white), Charcoal (#1F2937), Marigold Gold (white reverse), or high-key photography with an unobstructed quiet zone.

Incorrect usage

Do not stretch or compress proportions. Do not recolor rays or sun outside brand tokens. Do not add drop shadows, outer glows, or bevels. Do not rotate the A-frame. Do not place over cluttered backgrounds.
