04. Clear space & minimum size

Clear-space unit X = 0.12 × emblem width (provisional for symbol-only).

Symbol print: 20 mm min (25 mm preferred).
Symbol digital: 32 px min (48 px preferred).
Favicon: 16 / 24 / 32 px symbol-only.
Horizontal lockup: 220 px digital / 45 mm print.

07. Typography

Do not recreate the logo by typing Montserrat — wordmark lettering must be outlined vector paths in the master. Web scale: H1 48/36 · H2 32/28 · body 16/18 · never below 12 px for essential digital text. Spacing base: 8 px.

08. Digital & print production

  • Vector-first — SVG or print-ready PDF; never enlarge a small PNG for professional print.
  • PNG sizes — 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 180, 192, 256, 512, 1024 as needed.
  • Website header — horizontal lockup; symbol height typically 28–40 px.
  • Social avatar — symbol-only 1080×1080 with generous padding.
  • Bleed — confirm with printer; 3 mm common starting point in India.
  • Never let a vendor redraw the logo from a screenshot.
