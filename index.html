The house

Knowledge should travel as far as it is true.

Across Origin turns knowledge, stories and ideas into accessible content — books, media, training and translation — that can move across languages, formats and communities without losing its soul.

Credible. Thoughtful. Globally minded. Warm, modern, editorial and dependable.

The mark

An A-frame under a guiding origin.

Architectural frame, central source point, outward pathways and a curved global horizon — recognizable in one color, readable at small sizes, free of glow or texture.

01 · Origin circle

The source

A flat marigold point — the beginning of an idea, story or message. No glow.

02 · A-frame & pathways

Structure and travel

Balanced indigo legs and broad rays — ideas moving outward with intention.

03 · Global arc

Connection across nations

A single clean curve suggesting reach across regions — consistent stroke, no segments.
